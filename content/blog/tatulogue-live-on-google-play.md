Tatulogue is live on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tatulogue.app). Android users, this one's for you.

---

## What This Means

Android users can [download Tatulogue right now](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tatulogue.app) and start using it. That means browsing artists, finding your next piece, and following the shops and artists you actually care about, instead of hoping an algorithm surfaces them for you. If you're an artist, it means a presence built specifically for tattoo culture, not bolted onto a general-purpose feed.

Same app, same rules as iOS: no AI-generated content, no VC money behind it, search built around style, location, and actual posted work.

You can also use the [web version](https://app.tatulogue.com) right now if you'd rather stay in a browser.

## Both Platforms, Same Mission

Tatulogue is now live on iOS and Android. That was the whole point of building this: get it in front of as many artists and collectors as possible, on whatever device they're already using.

If you know a shop that wants to be featured, or you run one and want the details on getting set up, [reach out to us](/contact). Take a look at [who's already on board](/investors) if you want a sense of what that looks like.
