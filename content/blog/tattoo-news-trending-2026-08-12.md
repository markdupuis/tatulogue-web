## Jimothy the Raccoon Has Seattle Booked Solid

A raccoon named Jimothy — local Ballard mascot with a visible spinal difference — went viral, and now tattoo artists in Seattle are cashing in on the fame. Artist Julie Bolene of Slave to the Needle inked one of the first Jimothy pieces and has been doing one to three a week since. Her husband, artist Levi Walker Polzin, is also working the flash sheet. Merch — stickers, prints — followed. Clients are placing it wherever, including tender spots like the inner knee.

## Crypto Project Paid People to Tattoo Ads on Themselves

A memecoin project ran a bounty offering payment for a permanent tattoo of its ad. A Toronto artist going by "Jordan" took the deal, citing financial pressure, and has since said he plans to get it covered. The bounty drew criticism from researchers and public officials who said it targeted economically vulnerable people, since the payout was worth months of wages in the region it targeted (Chennai, India). The project's founder said he expected someone from a lower-income country to take the bounty.

## Alisa Liu's Baphomet-Style Lower Back Piece Sparked Backlash

Figure skater Alisa Liu has been addressing backlash over a lower-back tattoo that critics online compared to a baphomet symbol. She's responded directly to the criticism. The clip is circulating widely on TikTok under tattoo-controversy tags.

## Billie Eilish's Tattoo Removal Is Getting Attention Again

Clips referencing Billie Eilish's tattoo removal, and her comments connecting it to the Blohsh brand, are getting renewed traction on TikTok. Removal content in general — not just hers — is trending under the same tags right now, alongside pieces on regret and touch-up costs.

## Artist Community Rallies to Report a Toxic Shop Account

A tattoo-adjacent account accused of abusive behavior toward apprentices and clients got a coordinated reporting push from artists on Instagram. Multiple artists, including painter Jennifer Gennari, publicly thanked the community for the effort. The account's status is unconfirmed as of this writing.

## Ottawa Artist Launches a Run Club Off a Viral Post

A tattoo artist in Ottawa hosted the first "Slow Sunday" — a running meetup that grew out of a social post — aimed at building in-person friendships. CTV covered the first event.
