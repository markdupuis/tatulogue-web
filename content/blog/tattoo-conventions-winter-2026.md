> **TL;DR:** We confirmed 30 tattoo conventions from Nov 6, 2026 through Feb 28, 2027, each on the organizer's own site on Oct 12, 2026. November has eight our fall guide missed, December is thin in the US, and January and February are packed. Dates move, so recheck before you book a flight.

---

Tattoo conventions in November 2026 start stacking up fast. On the weekend of Nov 6 to 8, five shows we missed in our fall guide open in Tennessee, Indiana, California, Nebraska and Mississippi. Then the circuit rolls into winter: an exhibit hall in Wichita Falls, hotels in Rosemont and Austin, a convention center in Honolulu, a New Year's weekend show in Noblesville.

Picture a January Friday. Carpet, bad lighting, a hundred machines running, somebody's backpiece in progress behind a rope.

This is the follow-up to our [fall tattoo conventions guide](/blog/tattoo-conventions-fall-2026), which stops at Nov 22. Here are the November shows it missed, then everything we could confirm from December through February.

We checked each show's own website on Oct 12, 2026. Listing sites helped us find candidates, but nothing made the cut unless the organizer's site backed up the dates and city.

|---|---|---|---|
| Dec 4 to 6 | Wichita Falls Tattoo Expo ([Ink Masters](https://www.inkmasterstattooshow.com/)), TX | Ray Clymer Exhibit Hall | At the door; Friday listed at $20 |
| Dec 4 to 6 | [Tattoo Lab Project](https://www.tattoolabproject.com/), Naples, Italy | Mostra d'Oltremare | On sale; day one needs the 3-day pass |

## Why Do So Many Winter Shows Share the Same Names?

Because two touring circuits run 16 of the 30. [Villain Arts](https://villainarts.com/va-events/) runs eight (San Antonio, Tampa, Philadelphia, Minneapolis, San Diego, Maine, Jacksonville, Cleveland), and the [Ink Masters Tattoo Show](https://www.inkmasterstattooshow.com/) circuit runs another eight. Both circuits book back-to-back weekends in January and February.

[Villain Arts says](https://villainarts.com/faq/) its shows are all ages, with kids under 12 free. Its show pages run from 2 PM Friday to 8 PM Sunday, and refunds end 24 hours before the event starts. Most Ink Masters pages we read say tickets are sold at the door only, with $5 off before 5 PM on Friday. The circuit's contests charge $10 per tattoo to enter.

## How Do You Get the Most Out of a Winter Tattoo Convention?

Treat the floor as a place to meet artists and see work, and make the big decision later. Bring photo ID and cash, find out who's booked solid, and plan around winter weather.

**Wait on the big decisions.** This one is our read. A convention makes it easy to say yes to the first open chair. Grab cards, photograph the work, and sleep on any large piece. Our guides to [vetting an artist](/blog/how-to-vet-a-tattoo-artist) and [what tattoos cost](/blog/how-much-do-tattoos-cost) will help you sanity check a quote.

**Bring ID and cash.** Villain Arts says in its [FAQ](https://villainarts.com/faq/) that you must be 18 or older with a valid government-issued photo ID to get tattooed, no exceptions. Cash covers parking (Nashville's is cash only), door tickets and tips. [Vancouver](https://canadatattooshows.com/Vancouver-General-Info) takes cash and cards at the door and has ATMs in the hall. Our guide on [how much to tip a tattoo artist](/blog/how-much-to-tip-a-tattoo-artist) covers the math.

**Check the artist's books.** Villain Arts says many artists take walk-ups, while others book up completely in advance. For a specific artist or a bigger piece, message them before the show. Villain Arts and Vancouver both say an appointment doesn't replace a ticket, and admission doesn't count toward the tattoo.

**Pack for a long day.** [Vancouver's FAQ](https://canadatattooshows.com/Vancouver-FAQ) suggests water, snacks, a blanket, a pillow, earbuds or a book.

**Travel smart in winter.** Again, our read. Book a refundable room, leave a buffer day for a Maine or Minneapolis January, and plan how you'll look after a new tattoo on the road. The [aftercare guide](/blog/tattoo-aftercare-guide) is worth a read before you pack.

Winter is a good time to see a lot of work in one room. The calendar is thin in December, then fills fast from mid-January. Pick one show per weekend, check the organizer's page the week before, and go in with an artist already in mind. Keep any big piece off the floor until you've slept on it.

If you want to scout artists before you travel, browse by style on [Tatulogue](https://app.tatulogue.com). We checked these dates on Oct 12, 2026, and shows do change dates, venues and prices, so confirm on the official site before you buy anything. Then go enjoy the noise.

## FAQ

**What tattoo conventions are left in November 2026?**
Beyond the ones in our fall guide, we confirmed Middle Tennessee, Evansville, NorCal, Ink The Island and Inkin the Coast on Nov 6 to 8. San Antonio and Denver run Nov 13 to 15, and Tampa runs Nov 20 to 22.

**Are there tattoo conventions in the US in December 2026?**
We could confirm one: the Wichita Falls Tattoo Expo on Dec 4 to 6. Naples, Italy (Dec 4 to 6) and Bad Hersfeld, Germany (Dec 5 to 6) are the other December shows we confirmed.

**Which tattoo conventions are in January 2027?**
We confirmed ten, including Tattoos Saved My Life in Noblesville on Jan 1 to 3 and Vancouver on Jan 8 to 10. Minneapolis, Chattanooga and Honolulu all run Jan 15 to 17.

**What ID do you need to get tattooed at a convention?**
Villain Arts says you must be 18 or older with a valid government-issued photo ID, with no exceptions. Other shows set their own rules, so check the organizer's site.

**Do you need a ticket if you already have a tattoo appointment?**
Yes, at the shows that say so. Villain Arts and Vancouver both say everyone needs admission, and the ticket doesn't count toward the tattoo.
