Your feed, your inbox, your profile: all got some love this round. Here's what changed in 1.0.11.

## For Everyone

**Your feed now keeps loading as you scroll.** No more hitting a wall partway down.

**Signing in with Google is smoother**, and tells you what's wrong if it ever hiccups instead of just failing silently.

**Notifications now take you straight to the right post, comment, or profile** when you tap them.

**Double-tap to like**, because some habits shouldn't have to be relearned.

**Tag your own posts NSFW** so we can blur them appropriately for other users.

**New report options, including one for underage content**, so flagging something takes seconds.

**@ mention people in comments** and they'll get notified.

**Swipe straight through someone's posts from their profile grid** instead of hunting one at a time.

**Add links to your profile bio.**

**Tablet users get a real layout upgrade**, not just a stretched phone screen.
