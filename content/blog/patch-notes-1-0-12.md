Release 1.0.12 is all about the small things that get in your way. Here's what changed.

## Bug Fixes and Improvements

**Fixed a crash when uploading photos.** Posting a photo shouldn't take the app down with it.

**NSFW tagging now saves.** Tag your own posts NSFW and it sticks, so they're blurred properly for other users.

**Fixed Google sign-in getting stuck.** Signing in with Google now gets you through instead of leaving you hanging.

**Liking a post no longer jumps you to the top.** Like something and stay right where you were.

**Tag search finds tagged posts.** Search by a tag and the posts carrying it show up.

**Feed keeps loading as you scroll.** No more hitting a wall partway down.

**Double tap to like.**

**Links in your bio.**

**Comment mention notifications.** @ mention someone in a comment and they get notified.

**Fixed comment likes in Articles.**
