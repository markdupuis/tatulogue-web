8:T771,<p>Every tattoo tells two stories: the one the design represents, and the one of the artist who made it.</p>
<p>We're starting a series to tell the second kind. The Tatulogue Artist Spotlight is a recurring feature highlighting artists from our community — the paths they took to get here, the styles they've developed, and the work they're most proud of.</p>
<h2>Why We're Doing This</h2>
<p>Artists who work exclusively through social media are building on rented land. An algorithm change can cut their reach in half overnight. A platform policy shift can hide their content from the exact people looking for their style.</p>
<p>We built Tatulogue to give artists a more stable professional home — a platform designed specifically for tattoo work, not a general social feed where a tattoo portfolio competes with every other type of content.</p>
<p>But a platform is only as good as the people on it. So we want to introduce you to them.</p>
<h2>Who We're Looking For</h2>
<p>We're interested in the full range of what's happening in tattooing right now.</p>
<p>Artists who've been practising for decades. Artists who came up in apprenticeships and those who found their way in through other creative disciplines. Artists who specialise in styles with deep cultural roots. Artists developing something entirely new.</p>
<p>The common thread: work that's done with intention.</p>
<h2>Coming Up Next</h2>
<p>Our first proper spotlight is coming soon. If you're an artist on Tatulogue and want to be considered for a feature, reach out through the app.</p>
<p>In the meantime, explore artist profiles on Tatulogue and see the work being made right now:</p>
<p><a href="https://app.tatulogue.com">Browse artist portfolios →</a></p>
<hr>
<p><em>The Artist Spotlight series publishes monthly. We cover the full range of styles, backgrounds, and regions represented in the Tatulogue community.</em></p>
