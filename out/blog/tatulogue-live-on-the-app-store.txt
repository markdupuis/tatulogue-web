8:T7d8,<p>Tatulogue is live on the <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tatulogue/id6794140876" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">App Store</a>. That's it, that's the post, but we've got more to say.</p>
<hr>
<h2>What This Means</h2>
<p>iOS users can <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tatulogue/id6794140876" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">download Tatulogue right now</a> and start using it. If you're an enthusiast, that means browsing artists, finding your next piece, and following the shops and artists you care about in one place instead of scattered across Instagram algorithms and word of mouth. If you're an artist, it means a real presence built for tattoo culture specifically, not bolted on top of a general social feed.</p>
<p>You can also use the <a href="https://app.tatulogue.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">web version</a> right now if you're not on iOS or just prefer a browser.</p>
<h2>We Want Artists and Shops on Here</h2>
<p>This launch only means something if the artists are on it. We're actively looking for shops and artists who want in early, whether that's just claiming a profile or getting featured.</p>
<p>If you know a shop that wants to be featured, or you run one and want the details on getting set up, <a href="/contact">reach out to us</a>. We're also always talking to artists and industry partners about deeper involvement. Take a look at <a href="/investors">who's already on board</a> if you want a sense of what that looks like.</p>
<h2>Android Is Coming</h2>
<p>We know. We're working through beta steps on Android right now. No firm date yet, but it's actively in progress, not just on a someday list. If you want to know the second it's ready, <a href="/#early-access">get on the early access list</a> and we'll let you know.</p>
<hr>
<p>Thanks for being here this early. <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tatulogue/id6794140876" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Join us on iOS</a> and tell an artist you know.</p>
