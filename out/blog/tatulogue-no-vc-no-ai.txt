8:T8b1,<p>Most social platforms start with a pitch deck aimed at Silicon Valley. Ours started with a rule we wrote into the operator agreement before we took a single dollar: no venture capital, no big corporate money. Investment only comes from inside the tattoo industry.</p>
<hr>
<h2>Why We're Not Taking VC Money</h2>
<p>VC money comes with a growth-at-all-costs clock attached to it. Ad load goes up, engagement bait gets prioritized, and eventually the platform stops serving the people who built it and starts serving the return investors expect. We've all watched that happen to the apps we used to like.</p>
<p>We didn't want to build something that ends up there in five years. So the people funding Tatulogue are tattoo artists, shop owners, and industry partners, not venture funds. You can see who's actually backing us on our <a href="/investors">investors page</a>, and we've committed to giving back 50% of all profits to the tattoo community. That's not a marketing line, it's the actual structure of the business.</p>
<p><img src="/images/blog/real-tattoo-studio-artist.jpg" alt="A tattoo artist working in a warmly decorated studio surrounded by art"></p>
<h2>Why There's No AI</h2>
<p>Tatulogue has a standing rule: no AI content. Protect the art, protect the artist.</p>
<p>That means no AI-generated tattoo designs pushed into your feed, no AI slop crowding out actual work by actual tattooers. Feeds full of synthetic images are already a problem on the platforms artists are stuck using. We're not adding to it. If it's on Tatulogue, a person made it.</p>
<p><img src="/images/blog/real-hand-drawn-tattoo-design.jpg" alt="A tattoo artist hand-drawing a design in a studio"></p>
<h2>What This Actually Means for You</h2>
<p>If you're an artist, it means the platform funding you isn't the same platform trying to extract the most attention out of you. If you're a collector, it means what you're browsing is real work from real people, not filler.</p>
<p>We're building this to hand value back to the tattoo community, not to hand it up to outside investors. <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tatulogue/id6794140876" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Get the app</a> and see it for yourself.</p>
