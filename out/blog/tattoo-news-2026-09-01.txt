8:Td18,<p>Here's what's actually happening in tattoo culture this week: no takes, just what's happening, with sources.</p>
<hr>
<h2>Rep. Nancy Mace Went Public With Nine Tattoos She'd Kept Hidden for Years</h2>
<p>South Carolina Congresswoman Nancy Mace showed off nine tattoos on her YouTube show this month that she'd first disclosed in a February interview but never shown publicly. She got all nine in a single stretch between late 2023 and early 2024, during a period that included the end of her engagement and a wave of staff turnover in her office. Visible work includes the word "Justice" running down one forearm, a haloed figure, a sailboat, an owl, and a quote from Virginia Woolf's <em>Mrs. Dalloway</em>.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.newsweek.com/the-story-behind-nancy-maces-nine-new-tattoos-12330929" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Newsweek reports</a> she's connected the ink to surviving sexual assault, describing the tattoos as a way to "reclaim" her body. Her response to the online attention: "My biceps should NOT be a news story. We have more important things to worry about."</p>
<h2>A West Chester Artist Broke His Own Guinness World Record</h2>
<p>Brannan Clift, owner of Liberty Bell Tattoo &#x26; Piercing in West Chester, Pennsylvania, spent 76 straight hours tattooing the same person, breaking a record previously held by a two-person team from Italy, and beating his own earlier 52-hour mark in the process. His canvas was a friend, Christian Eshbaugh, who Clift has called one of the most pain-tolerant people he knows. <a href="https://vista.today/2026/07/west-chester-tattoo-artist-guinness-world-record/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Vista.Today has the story</a>.</p>
<h2>Colorado Springs Hosted Its Villain Arts Tattoo Festival</h2>
<p>The Villain Arts Tattoo Festival ran August 28 through 30 at the Norris-Penrose Event Center, pulling in artists from across the country including Kelsey Lorrae, Carlos Piedrahita, Loco Dharma, and Horihachi. <a href="https://gazette.com/2026/08/29/colorado-springs-tattoo-expo-is-a-sensuous-feast-of-ink-and-art/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">The Colorado Springs Gazette covered the weekend</a>, alongside the usual convention mix of live tattooing, contests, and vendor booths.</p>
<h2>Two of Europe's Biggest Conventions Land This Weekend</h2>
<p>If you're near London or Amsterdam this weekend, both cities have a major show running September 4 through 6. The <a href="https://www.skiddle.com/news/all/UKs-biggest-tattoo-show-returns-to-London-in-September/61856/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Big London Tattoo Show</a> takes over ExCel London with around 450 artists working everything from neo-trad to blackout. Meanwhile Amsterdam hits its 20th year with the Amsterdam Tattoo Convention, bringing in more than 350 national and international artists to NDSM.</p>
