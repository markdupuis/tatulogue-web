There is no easy way to find a tattoo artist by style and location. And the social media landscape is in need of a mixup. We're setting out to do both by creating the first alternative social media platform with features designed for tattooers.

With a rule of no AI content. Protect the art, protect the artist.

Social media platforms today have become billion-dollar machines that cater only to investors. We believe in People over Profit — and that there's a better way for social media to function, by enhancing our lives and giving back to the community.

So we have a commitment to give back 50% of all profits back to the tattoo community.
