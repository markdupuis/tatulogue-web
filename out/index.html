Getting a tattoo is exciting!

But………………

The process of finding the right artist can be… challenging.

The challenge for tattoo artists…

Tattoo artists face a lot of challenges when it comes to getting new customers in the door.

Having to market themselves non-stop, battling with mega influencers on social media from sports, to politics, to brands, tech companies, podcasts… you name it.

And without a massive following you're stuck in a never-ending cycle of trying to get your artwork seen.

The challenge for enthusiasts…

Shopping for an artist is anything but easy. You find something that you want to get tattooed. The next step is finding an artist that specializes in that style. Where do you start?

You do a search online for shops near you and start looking at random artists' portfolios. You go to social media and start looking for tattoo artists. You ask in groups and pages for recommendations. You ask for recommendations from friends and family.

There is no easy way to find tattoo artists by style that can help bring your vision to reality.

Your tattoo journey should be MUCH easier.

And THAT is why we are creating

Tatulogue.

The place to find your tattoo inspiration and find the perfect artist to do that tattoo. And share and enjoy tattoo culture in an InkSocial environment.

Dedicated accounts

At launch, Tatulogue will feature two profile types. One for licensed Tattoo Artists, and one for clients and everyone else called an “Enthusiast” profile.
